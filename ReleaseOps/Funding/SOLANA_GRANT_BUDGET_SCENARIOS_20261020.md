## Cash-cost policy
Default external cash cost is $0 until free/open-source/existing capacity is exhausted. Devnet has no mainnet liquidity requirement. Any later device-lab, hosting, observability, audit, or legal cost must be a separately evidenced line item; token purchases, liquidity, listings, paid influencers and speculative treasury costs are excluded.

## Decision rule
Prefer the smallest milestone scope that still produces a reusable public good and independently verifiable acceptance evidence. Do not submit a dollar request until (1) rate basis evidence exists, (2) current live application requirements are rechecked, (3) public repo/demo and devnet evidence are linked, and (4) legal/privacy review scope is recorded.
