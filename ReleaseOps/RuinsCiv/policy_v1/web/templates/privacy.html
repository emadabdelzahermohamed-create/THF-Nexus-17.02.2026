RuinsCiv Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026.

RuinsCiv is a social-world game available through Android and the web. This policy describes the data used by the RuinsCiv service. The Android app and web client use the same account and backend service.

Data we process

  • Account and security data such as username, account identifiers, authentication/session records and linked sign-in provider identifiers.
  • Profile and game data such as avatar choices, settings, language, friends, progression, inventory, property, jobs, quests and virtual-world position.
  • Social content such as messages and user-provided media only when those features are used.
  • Technical and service data needed for reliability, abuse prevention, security, diagnostics and authoritative game-state validation.

Why we use data

We use data to provide accounts, synchronize game state, operate social and gameplay features, secure the service, prevent cheating and abuse, respond to support or deletion requests, and maintain service reliability.

OAuth and external providers

If you choose an external sign-in provider, RuinsCiv processes the provider identifier and the minimum profile information returned by the provider for account linking. A provider is not considered active merely because an adapter exists in source code; only providers enabled in the production configuration are used.

Location and device sensors

Virtual-world coordinates are game-state data and are not GPS location. RuinsCiv does not claim to collect device location, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, health/fitness data or payment data unless those capabilities are present and enabled in the exact production release. Any later release that adds such collection must update this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration before release.

Sharing and processors

Data sent to the RuinsCiv backend is used to provide the RuinsCiv service. Third-party processors are disclosed when they are actually enabled in production. We do not treat a planned or disabled integration as an active data-sharing destination.

Security and retention

RuinsCiv uses authenticated sessions, server-authoritative state for sensitive game actions, transport encryption on production endpoints, access controls and operational safeguards. Data is retained only as needed for the service, security, fraud/abuse prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Retention that must continue after account deletion is limited to what is required and is not used to recreate the deleted account.

Account deletion

You can request permanent deletion from inside RuinsCiv or through the external account deletion page. Deletion removes the account and associated game data subject to any narrowly required retention described above.

Children and regional requirements

Age eligibility, parental controls and regional requirements are applied according to the final distribution configuration and applicable law. RuinsCiv does not use sensitive traits for targeted advertising.

Contact

Use the support contact displayed in the RuinsCiv application and official store listing. Production contact and controller details must match the verified Play developer profile before public release.
