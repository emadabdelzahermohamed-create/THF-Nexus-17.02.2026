## The category

**THF is not a “fitness coin.” It is the planned coordination layer for a Verified Human Activity Network.**

TopHeroFit is already building the product-side primitive that makes the category possible: **Proof-of-Workout**. Health-provider provenance, phone-sensor evidence and anti-cheat controls create verifiable activity outcomes without putting raw health, biometric or precise-location data on-chain.

The long-term network connects four economic actors that normally live in disconnected systems:

1. athletes and everyday users producing real verified effort;
2. gyms/coaches operating training, memberships and challenges;
3. sponsors/brands funding measurable participation campaigns;
4. games/apps consuming signed proof-of-activity eligibility without needing raw health data.

That makes sport and gaming composable around **verified human effort**, rather than unverifiable clicks, self-reported workouts, or isolated game economies.

## Why this can change sports + gaming

Today a workout in one app is usually a dead-end data point. A gaming reward is usually disconnected from real-world effort. A sponsor challenge often measures registrations or clicks instead of trustworthy physical participation. A gym has little interoperable infrastructure for verified cross-gym/community competitions.

THF's thesis is that Proof-of-Workout can become a reusable verification rail: a run, training session or challenge can be validated once and then produce privacy-preserving eligibility/receipts that approved downstream systems can consume.

Examples after product/legal gates are satisfied:

- verified city/gym/friend competitions;
- real-world fitness quests that unlock game-native rewards;
- sponsor campaigns paid against verified participation rather than impressions alone;
- gym leagues and cross-gym challenges;
- B2B APIs for sports/gaming apps that need proof of real activity;
- an ecosystem treasury/community layer that can finance integrations, competitions and public-good verifier development.

## Token role — design hypothesis, not a public promise

The token must earn its place in the network. Candidate utility is being modelled around real product demand:

- partner/API credits for Proof-of-Workout verification and competition infrastructure;
- partner/sponsor/gym campaign settlement where lawful;
- bonding/staking for service actors only where a real security/slashing function exists;
- ecosystem governance and grants once decentralisation is meaningful;
- cross-application coordination while ordinary game currencies and fitness points stay separate.

THF Token will not silently convert from game currencies or ordinary activity points, and a phone-local workout result cannot authorize treasury transfers.
