## Objective

Build a real market around THF Token without relying on artificial volume, misleading return promises, or an under-capitalized public launch. THF is positioned as the coordination/economic layer for a verified-human-activity network spanning fitness, gyms, competitions and interoperable gaming rewards. The product differentiator is Proof-of-Workout: activity provenance and anti-cheat make sports/gaming incentives measurable rather than trust-based.

THF Token remains separate from ordinary fitness points and game-native currencies. A workout, local phone assertion, or game balance cannot directly authorize a treasury transfer.

## Market narrative

Core category: **Verified Human Activity / Proof-of-Workout Network**.

THF should not be marketed as “a fitness coin.” The launch story is that TopHeroFit turns real, verified human activity into a reusable coordination primitive for sports, gyms, sponsors, competitions and games. The token is planned as an ecosystem coordination and settlement rail only where legally/store-policy cleared; product usage must retain value even before token launch.

Do not promise token price appreciation, exchange listings, guaranteed yields, or investment returns. Quantitative traction claims require evidence.

## Stage 1 — UAE issuer readiness

Choose one issuer/regulatory home after counsel review:

### Dubai / VARA route

Treat issuance as regulated until confirmed otherwise. Determine Category 1/Category 2/exempt classification from actual token rights and business model. For a normal transferable utility token, prepare on the conservative assumption that Category 2 requirements apply, including current VARA/distributor approval process, licensed distribution/placement, whitepaper, issuer disclosures, AML/CFT, technical/security/privacy and cryptographic controls.

### Abu Dhabi / ADGM route

Use ADGM/FSRA analysis if the instrument or financing structure is better suited to ADGM. Security-like or investment-like rights require securities/financial-services analysis and cannot be relabelled as utility merely by wording.

No public TGE or irreversible fundraising instrument is executed until issuer/entity, regulatory classification, purchaser restrictions and custody/treasury controls are cleared.
