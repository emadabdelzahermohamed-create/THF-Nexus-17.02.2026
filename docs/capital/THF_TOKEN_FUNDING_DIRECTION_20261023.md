# THF — Token Funding Direction

Updated: 2026-10-23. Working specification, not a live offering or investment invitation.
Public identity: Top Hero Fit. Contact: TopHeroFit@gmail.com.

## Current focus
Only Fitness delivery and token-based ecosystem financing are active priorities. Games development is parked without abandoning the roadmap. Funding means a verified contribution/settlement or a documented financing commitment; research, drafts and scheduled work are not funds raised.

## Brand positioning
Top Hero Fit is building an interconnected ecosystem that makes learning, physical activity and gaming more enjoyable through challenges, verifiable achievement and clearly governed rewards. Fitness is the current product foundation; education and cross-game integrations are roadmap items. THF token utility is a design under validation, intended to coordinate eligible ecosystem participation and partner-funded programs rather than promise passive investment returns. Rewards depend on eligibility, anti-fraud checks, available campaign budgets and published terms. Financial token participation must not be marketed to minors; youth-friendly learning/game experiences can use separate non-financial achievements.

## Evidence boundaries
Fitness 51009 was built, published to Play Internal and installed from Play on a physical SM-A217F during the 2026-10-23 recovery. A short Home/resume test retained foreground service and accelerometer/gyroscope registration. This is not public Production, complete Health Connect verification, a completed education platform or a live interoperable game economy. Recheck newer product state before each publication.

## Capital mechanisms under evaluation
| Route | Possible benefit | Blocking verification |
|---|---|---|
| Metaplex Genesis Presale / Launch Pool | Solana-native token distribution with fixed price or discovery | Existing mint compatibility; permissions; fees; full sale lifecycle; allocation and refund rules |
| Raydium LaunchLab | Bonding-curve proceeds can migrate to CPMM liquidity at graduation | Existing-token/authority compatibility; creation costs; graduation threshold; LP ownership/lock policy |
| PinkSale Solana | Token-sale setup and liquidity features | Actual existing-mint support, fees, founder privacy/KYC and token authority requirements |
| Fjord | Fixed/tiered/LBP sale; documented Zero Liquidity LBP avoids initial paired collateral | Solana support for the exact mechanism, token compatibility, all fees and eligibility; not assumed |
| Strategic future-token financing | Potential operating capital before public distribution | Stage fit, terms, issuer/investor eligibility and confidential diligence |
| Juicebox | Transparent programmable community treasury on supported EVM chains | Not a drop-in SPL sale; avoid chain migration or duplicate mint without explicit design decision |

No upfront paired liquidity does not mean no gas/fees or guaranteed buyer demand. A successful sale does not itself establish sustainable secondary-market liquidity.

## Launch package
- Litepaper: present product vs roadmap, problem, audience, utility, risks and limits.
- Exact token register: chain/mint/supply/decimals/authorities and holder distribution verified on-chain.
- Allocation and vesting: reconcile 100% of supply; disclose insiders and unlock dates.
- Milestone roadmap: Fitness trust/background acceptance, transparent competition pilot, partner reward integration, learning pilot, then expanded gaming utility.
- Use of proceeds: operating runway, security, distribution requirements, ecosystem budgets and LP allocation separately stated.
- Liquidity policy: paired assets, LP ownership, lock terms, withdrawal powers, minimum raise and failure/refund behavior.
- Acquisition plan: product-led community and truthful demos; no fabricated traction or paid engagement presented as organic.
- Brand-only public contact and disclosures; private required diligence handled separately.

## Privacy and execution
Do not publish founder name, face, personal mailbox, precise location or identity-bearing repository links in campaign materials. Existing public account history and on-chain transactions may be linkable; do not promise total anonymity. Use truthful confidential verification when required, never substitute invented identity or incorporation. Do not assume a platform takes over all issuer obligations. Preparatory documentation, technical validation and non-binding route discussions may proceed now; accepting money must follow the actual mechanism's requirements and a reviewed transaction specification.

Keep game XP, virtual goods and THF balances separate. Do not silently convert them or imply a redemption right. Do not spend funds designated for LP on operating subscriptions. No guaranteed returns, appreciation, listings or investor exit.
