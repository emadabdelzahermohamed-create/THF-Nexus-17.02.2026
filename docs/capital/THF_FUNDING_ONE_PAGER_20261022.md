## What TopHeroFit is

TopHeroFit is a working Android fitness platform evolving into a verified fitness + gaming + gym operating ecosystem. The core technical primitive is **Proof-of-Workout**: provenance-aware activity verification built from Health Connect and device-sensor evidence, anti-cheat policy, and privacy-preserving attestations.

The consumer product combines:

- verified activity and anti-cheat;
- adaptive training;
- high-quality offline exercise instruction;
- voice coaching and licensed workout music;
- verified social competitions and marathons;
- gym occupancy, memberships, booking, coaches, programs and analytics;
- signed eligibility claims that games can consume without converting game currencies into THF Token.

## Solana thesis

Raw health, biometric and precise-location data should remain off-chain. Solana is used for low-cost commitments/receipts and interoperable verification outcomes so other apps can verify eligible human activity without receiving raw personal evidence.

A reusable verifier can support sports, games, wellness, learning or other proof-of-human-effort applications independently of THF Token economics.
