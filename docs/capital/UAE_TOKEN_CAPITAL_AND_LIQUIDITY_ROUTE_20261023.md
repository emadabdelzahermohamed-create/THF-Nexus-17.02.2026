## Treasury-to-market rule
A successful raise is not itself market liquidity. The approved launch budget must explicitly allocate separately for:
- operating runway/product/security;
- legal/entity/audit/compliance;
- ecosystem incentives with caps/vesting;
- initial DEX LP inventory;
- professional market-maker inventory/retainer if used;
- contingency.
Never drain operating runway merely to make the pool look deep.
