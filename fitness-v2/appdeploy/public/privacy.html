THF Fitness Privacy Policy

Effective: October 3, 2026.

Top Hero Fit Fitness uses account identity information supplied by the sign-in provider and fitness information you choose to record, such as workouts, sets, repetitions, load, rest, effort, sport and progress, to provide optional cross-device synchronization.

Offline and Health Connect data

The exercise library, training profile and workout log work offline. Health Connect steps, exercise sessions, distance and active calories are read on the device to show a seven-day summary. This version does not send those Health Connect summary values to the THF Fitness server. Completed THF workouts may be written to Health Connect after permission is granted.

Account synchronization

Account sign-in and server synchronization are optional. When enabled, completed THF workout records are sent over HTTPS and scoped to the authenticated account. Short-lived Android access tokens are held in memory and are not stored in app preferences.

Sharing and sale

Data may be processed by infrastructure and identity providers needed to operate the service. THF Fitness does not sell personal data and does not include an advertising SDK in this Android build.

Retention and deletion

You can start deleting THF Fitness account data from Profile in the Android app or from Account & privacy in the web app. The bounded deletion flow removes app-owned goals, workout history, Android sessions, synchronization/idempotency records and competition submissions. It may require more than one request for a large account.

AppDeploy provides the sign-in identity used by this deployment. Its published app SDK does not currently expose a user-identity deletion method, so the THF route deletes app-owned account data and signs the app session out; it does not claim to delete an external identity-provider account.

For instructions, see Account deletion.
