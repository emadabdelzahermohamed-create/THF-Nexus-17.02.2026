Delete THF Fitness account data

  1. Open the THF Fitness web app.
  2. Sign in with the same account used for Android synchronization.
  3. Open Account & privacy, choose Delete account data, and confirm.
  4. Keep the page open until it reports completion. A large account may require repeated bounded requests.

The flow removes app-owned goals, legacy and V2 workout history, Android authentication sessions and tickets, synchronization/idempotency records, and competition submissions. After completion the web session is signed out.

A Google Play purchase remains associated with the purchasing Google Play account so that the cosmetic entitlement can be restored. Deleting THF Fitness account data does not cancel, refund or erase Google Play order records; purchase and refund management remains available through Google Play.

The flow does not delete your Google, Apple, X, email-provider, or shared AppDeploy identity. Manage those identities with their respective providers.
